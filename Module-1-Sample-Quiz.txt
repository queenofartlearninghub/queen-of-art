1. What is Canva?
A. A spreadsheet program
B. An online design tool for making posts, flyers, and presentations
C. A video calling app
D. An antivirus program
Answer: B

2. Which is the quickest way to start a design from a ready-made layout?
A. Delete all the elements first
B. Install a plugin
C. Choose a template
D. Print a blank page
Answer: C

3. Where do you go in Canva to save your design as a PDF or PNG file?
A. Share, then Download
B. Home, then Settings
C. Apps, then Charts
D. Edit, then Undo
Answer: A

4. Which file format supports a transparent background?
A. JPG
B. MP4
C. DOCX
D. PNG
Answer: D

5. Which format keeps your layout fixed when you send a flyer to a printer?
A. GIF
B. PDF
C. MP3
D. TXT
Answer: B

6. Where do you find photos, elements, text, and uploads while you edit a design?
A. In your browser history
B. In your phone settings
C. In the side panel of the editor
D. In the Windows taskbar
Answer: C

7. What does the Undo button do?
A. It reverses your last change
B. It deletes the design permanently
C. It shares the design
D. It changes the language
Answer: A

8. What happens to your design while you work in Canva?
A. It is emailed to you every hour
B. It is deleted when you close the tab
C. It is printed automatically
D. It is saved automatically to your account
Answer: D

9. Why is it helpful to start from a template?
A. It stops you from editing the text
B. It saves time and gives you a professional layout you can customize
C. It removes all your images
D. It makes the file unreadable
Answer: B

10. Why should you choose the right design type, such as Instagram Post, at the start?
A. It changes your password
B. It adds music to your design
C. It sets the correct size for that platform
D. It blocks downloads
Answer: C
